7. Limitations

  • PRISM output supports an investigator's decision. It does not identify a vehicle on its own.
  • Characters shown as ? could not be read with at least 0.7 confidence, confirmed by the second OCR model, and backed by a clear multi-frame majority. PRISM never fills them in.
  • Colour and body type come from a zero-shot vision-language model and can be wrong under glare, night lighting or occlusion.
  • Uncertain attributes add nothing to the score.
  • The registry is synthetic. Scores rank candidates for review; they are not probabilities of identity.
  • The advisor’s leads are frames to check by eye, not evidence.
